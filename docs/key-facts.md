# Key IoT Facts & Figures (2025–2026)

A curated, **sourced** reference of the most-cited statistics in IoT. Every figure links to its primary source. Because market-research firms use different methodologies, ranges are given where estimates diverge — always cite the figure *with* its source and year.

> **Methodology note:** "IoT market size" varies by an order of magnitude across firms depending on whether the figure counts only devices, only software/services, or the full stack (hardware + connectivity + platforms + industrial automation). Treat any single number with skepticism; prefer ranges and name the source.

## How to Use These Figures

- **Always cite the source and year** — a 2025 figure may be obsolete within months.
- **Prefer ranges over single points** for market size (methodologies differ wildly).
- **Distinguish "devices" from "market value"** — they measure different things.
- **Primary sources first** — link to the research firm or standards body, not aggregators.
